Who it is for

Deeptest Analytics is for engineering and QA teams inside software companies. These are the people who own whether a change is safe to ship.

QA writes and reviews cases, watches the suite, and is usually first to see that a failure is real. Engineering uses the same record when a change breaks a case, or when a case no longer matches the product. A lead uses the report when a release needs a plain account of what was tested.

It is not a general dashboard. It is the place those groups look at the same cases, the same runs, and the same account of what failed. A reviewer should not have to rebuild that from a log and a chat thread.

It fits a team that already has a way to run tests and needs the design of those tests, and the reading of the results, to stay with the runs.

  • QA Cases, reviews, and the health of the suite.
  • Engineering When a change breaks a case, or a case has gone stale.
  • Leads When a release needs a clear account of what was tested.
  • The team One record, instead of a log plus a separate write-up.
