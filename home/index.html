The accessibility audit an expert would do by hand

The Accessibility Audit Application checks your website against WCAG 2.2 with a real keyboard, screen-reader output and measured evidence. It runs on your own computer, and it tells you plainly what it could not check.

Why it is different

Evidence you can stand behind

Most automated checkers read a page's code and stop there. This one also operates the page, measures what it sees, and keeps the doubtful cases apart from the certain ones.

Real interaction

It uses the page like a person does

It presses Tab through every stop, looks for a visible focus ring, measures contrast from the rendered pixels, checks target sizes and reflow at 320 pixels, and can read the page with NVDA or VoiceOver.

Honest coverage

It says what it did not check

Every WCAG 2.2 level A and AA criterion is marked as checked, partly checked or needing a person. A clean result never passes for a criterion nobody tested, and the rest comes as a checklist.

Private by design

Your pages stay on your machine

Audits run locally, so staging sites and pages behind a login are never uploaded. There is no account and no license server.

How an audit runs

From a list of pages to a report you can hand over

  1. 01

    Choose pages

    Give it addresses, a sitemap or a starting page to crawl, or record a journey through a login.

  2. 02

    Audit

    Two rule engines run together with its own keyboard, visual, form and screen-reader checks.

  3. 03

    Review

    Each finding shows the element, a cropped screenshot and how to fix it. Confirm it or dismiss it with a note.

  4. 04

    Report

    One self-contained report for people, and machine-readable results for a build pipeline.

Our own website first

It found 12 problems on this site

Before writing these pages we audited the previous version of this website. On four pages the app reported 12 serious violations: a logo link whose spoken name did not match its visible text, and groups of cards a screen reader could not tell apart.

We fixed them before publishing this version. The automated checks now report no violations here, and the criteria that need a person are on our own review list too.
