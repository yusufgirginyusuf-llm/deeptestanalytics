One connected workflow

From intended behavior to an accountable result

Tests are most useful when their design, execution, and interpretation stay together. The planned framework follows that full lifecycle.

  1. 01

    Design

    Define behavior, setup, and the result that counts as a pass.

  2. 02

    Run

    Keep an exact record of what passed, failed, or never started.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    Group related failures and distinguish signal from noise.

  4. 04

    Report

    Give reviewers a concise account grounded in the run itself.

A shared record

Less to reconstruct after the run

Teams often have the execution data but still need to reconstruct what it means across logs, tickets, and chat threads.

Deeptest Analytics is designed to preserve the relationship between a case, the product behavior it checks, the runs it appears in, and the finding a reviewer eventually shares.
