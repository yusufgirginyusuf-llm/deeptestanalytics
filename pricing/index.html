Good to know

What you can rely on

  • Your reports stay yours. When a trial or a license ends, new audits stop. Everything you audited before stays readable.
  • No account and no license server. The license key is checked on your computer. Nothing about your audits is sent to us.
  • Early access means unsigned installers. Until our installers are code-signed, Windows and macOS show a security prompt during installation. We will tell you how to proceed.
  • It does not certify conformance. Automated checks cover part of WCAG. The app tells you which part.
